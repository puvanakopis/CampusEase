# Booking Cancellation Policy

## Policy Overview
- **Cancellation Window:** Up to 24 hours before scheduled time
- **Cancellation Method:** Student dashboard → My Bookings
- **Refund Timeframe:** 5-7 working days for paid bookings

## Cancellation Process
1. Navigate to "My Bookings"
2. Select booking (Accommodation/Vehicle)
3. Click "Cancel Booking"
4. Provide reason
5. Confirm cancellation

## Important Notes
- Provider cancellations: Immediate student notification + full refund
- No student action needed for provider-initiated cancellations
- All cancellations must be done through the platform
